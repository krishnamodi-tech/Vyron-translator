🏆 Hackathon Command Center

The step-by-step checklist to ensure Team Vyron wins 1st Place.

🔥 1. The Live Demo (Flawless)

Print a REAL Government Notice

Don't scan a blank screen. Print a highly confusing Hindi/English public notice on physical paper. Scan it live using the phone camera to prove it works in the real world.

Showcase the 3D & Voice

Make sure the room is quiet enough, or hold the mic to the phone when you click "Play Audio". Let the judges hear the app speaking the regional language.

🎤 2. The Pitch (Storytelling)

Start with the "Mom Test"

Ask the judges: "Have you ever seen your parents struggle with a tax or banking notice?" Make the problem deeply personal and relatable before showing the code.

The Google Translate Attack

Explicitly say: "Google Translate just gives you complex Hindi words instead of complex English words. We extract ACTION. We tell them what to do next."

💡 3. Future Scope (Defending Q&A)

Pitch WhatsApp Integration

When asked "How will villagers download this app?", answer: "Our next phase is a WhatsApp Bot. You just send a photo of the document on WhatsApp, and the AI replies with an audio voice note explaining it."

Defend the LLM API Cost

If asked about AI costs, say: "Models like Gemini Flash are becoming incredibly cheap. Plus, we aim to partner with Common Service Centres (CSCs) or NGOs to subsidize it as a public utility."
